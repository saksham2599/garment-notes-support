Garment Notes

Garment Notes support

Keep careful customer measurements and garment work sheets on one device.

Getting started

Add a customer, optionally create a named measurement profile, then create one job for each physical garment. Enter exact instructions and measurement conventions. Changes at a fitting should be saved as a new revision.

Why can’t I mark a job Ready?

Record a shop check of the latest work sheet first. A new fitting revision requires a new check. This is a shop record, not an authenticated customer signature.

Moving to a new device

Export a complete backup in Shop and save it somewhere private. On the new device, choose Restore from backup, review the counts, and confirm replacement. Separate devices do not sync, and restore does not merge shops.

Recovery

Restore keeps a copy of the previous shop. Open Shop > Recovery copies to export it, then choose Restore from backup to use that exported copy. Do not uninstall the app to troubleshoot missing records.

Contact

Open a support issue on GitHub. A GitHub account is required to post. Issues are public; describe the problem without customer data or private information. Include the app version, iOS version and the steps that caused the issue. Do not include real customer details in screenshots.
