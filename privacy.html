Garment Notes privacy policy

Effective date: 13 September 2026. This policy covers the planned first release with optional Pro and advertising. Owner: Saksham Goyal.

Garment Notes is an offline customer and garment records app. We do not operate a service that collects the customers, contacts, measurements, instructions or photos you save in the app. The free version uses Google AdMob for banner advertising and Google’s User Messaging Platform for privacy choices. There is no developer-operated customer database or analytics service.

Advertising and privacy choices

Google may process IP addresses (including approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, crash reports and performance diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Customer records, contact details, measurements, work instructions and garment photos are not passed to the advertising SDK. We request non-personalized ads and disable Google’s publisher first-party identifier and advertising profile personalization. Before starting advertising, we ask for Apple’s tracking permission. If you allow it, Google may use the device advertising identifier to deliver and measure ads. If you decline, advertising stays off and all shop features remain free and usable. These choices do not mean that advertising collects no data.

Where required, Google’s consent form appears before advertising. Use Shop > Ad privacy choices to change available choices. If consent cannot be obtained or ads cannot load, shop features remain usable. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses advertising data.

Pro purchases

Garment Notes Pro is an optional monthly auto-renewing subscription that removes ads while active. Apple handles payment and subscription management. The app checks Apple-verified transaction status to enable Pro; we do not receive your payment-card details. Restore Purchases is available on the Pro screen. Canceling Pro does not delete or restrict your shop records. Buying Pro stops new ad placements; it does not erase information already processed by Google. See Apple’s privacy policy and the Apple Standard EULA.

Your records

Records are stored in the app’s protected local directory. iOS device backups, including iCloud Backup if you enable it, may include app data according to your device settings. Separate devices do not sync through Garment Notes.

Photos and reminders

You choose reference photos with Apple’s system photo picker. The app saves resized JPEG copies without location metadata and leaves the originals in your photo library untouched. Optional reminders are local notifications with generic text that does not include customer details.

Sharing and backups

You control exports through Apple’s sharing and Files interfaces. PDF work sheets include the customer name, garment details and measurements; they exclude contact details, private customer notes and photos. Complete backups include all records and garment photos. Exported files are not encrypted by Garment Notes. Choose a private storage location and control access to those files.

Deletion and recovery

Archive hides a record without deleting it. Deleting a garment removes that job, its work-sheet history and photos from the active database. Deleting a customer removes the customer’s records and all linked garments and photos. A pre-restore recovery copy is kept when you restore a backup; those copies can be exported or deleted in Shop > Recovery copies. Previously exported files and operating-system backups are separate copies that you must manage separately. Uninstalling the app can remove local records.

Support

For support, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public: do not include private customer information, contact details or backups. If you contact support, provide only the information needed to explain the issue. Avoid sending customer records or backups unless necessary and agreed.

This website and support

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information and support issue content under its privacy statement. We add no analytics or advertising scripts. Information you voluntarily include in a support issue is used to respond to it and is publicly visible on GitHub.

Changes

If app behavior changes, this policy will be updated before release of features that affect data handling.
